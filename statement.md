# Project Statement

## 1. Problem Statement

Students often need to keep track of their attendance across different subjects and determine whether they are meeting the required attendance criteria. Manually maintaining attendance records can be inconvenient and makes it difficult to estimate how many future classes need to be attended.

The **Attendance Tracker with Prediction** project provides a simple system for recording, viewing, and analysing attendance. It also provides basic attendance predictions using the available attendance data, helping students understand their current attendance status and plan their future attendance accordingly.

## 2. Scope of the Project

The project focuses on maintaining attendance records for different subjects and providing useful attendance-related information through a simple menu-driven system.

The scope of the project includes:

- Adding and maintaining subject-wise attendance information.
- Viewing the current attendance percentage.
- Calculating attendance-related statistics.
- Providing basic predictions based on attendance data.
- Using a machine learning model for attendance prediction.
- Storing attendance information for future use.
- Providing a simple and user-friendly interaction through the application.

The project is intended for academic attendance management and prediction and can be extended in the future with additional features such as graphical dashboards, notifications, and more advanced prediction methods.

## 3. Target Users

The primary target users of the project are:

- **College Students:** To record and monitor their subject-wise attendance.
- **Students at Risk of Short Attendance:** To understand their current attendance situation and estimate future attendance requirements.
- **Academic Users:** To analyse attendance information in a simple and organized manner.

## 4. High-Level Features

### 1. Subject Management
Users can add subjects and maintain attendance information for different subjects.

### 2. Attendance Tracking
The system records attended and total classes and calculates the corresponding attendance percentage.

### 3. Attendance Viewing
Users can view their current attendance information for the subjects stored in the system.

### 4. Basic Prediction
The system provides attendance-related predictions based on the recorded attendance data.

### 5. AI/ML Prediction
A machine learning model is used to analyse attendance data and provide a prediction based on the available information.

### 6. Data Storage
Attendance records are stored so that the information can be accessed and updated during future use.

## 5. Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to develop a simple attendance management system that combines attendance tracking with prediction. The project aims to reduce the effort involved in manually calculating attendance and provide students with useful information for managing their attendance effectively.
